### 2. MT-262 (c) — the orange arrows: which arrows?

### 3. Two meaning changes you should see before validating

**The covered-track indicator now means something different.** It was "this track is blocked" — the whole
edge, matching the routing guard. It is now "the train is here" — as long as the train, on the road it is
on. That follows your words, and the mark is a strict subset of what the railway blocks, so the picture
never claims more protection than exists. But it now claims **less** than the railway enforces, which is
the reverse of the old rule.

**The length guard now binds on autonomy too.** Removing the fence (below) means the rule applies to every
tier, not only manual sends. `isPathClear` is the tier every door passes through, and `behaviour.md` §1
makes autonomy the stricter tier, so refusing only the operator would have inverted it. Journeys that were
legal yesterday may be refused today.

### MT-337 (2) — the home locomotive drawn twice

A station may legitimately carry two captions — a long platform labelled at both ends — and each caption

caption away, `migrateStationLabels` writes through the raw store door and does not. `1 - Main:6,4` on your
railway is captioned on `6,5` and on `6,4`. A *self*-caption now gives way when another square already
names that station; two captions at opposite ends of a platform are both offset, so neither is touched.
