# What to look at when you're back

Twenty-three numbered items in five stages, in the order I would do them. Stage A is the only one that
cannot wait; the rest can be picked up whenever.

Roughly: A 30 min · B 15 min · C 20 min · D 10 min · E 30 min.

---

## Stage A — Run it (30 min)

Nothing below substitutes for this. Open a layout, `Autonomy` → `Add a Configuration...`, place two
trains, press start.

**1. Watch trains run for five minutes.** Nothing should stop silently or take a route you would not
have chosen.

**2. Press Graceful Stop.** Everything should halt, and the buttons should come back promptly — not
instantly, but within a train's arrival. *This changed: see item 16.*

**3. Hover "No available paths"** on a locomotive that cannot move. You should get a list of stations
and, for each, the reason. This is new and it is the feature I would most like your read on.

**4. Open the setup editor → "Why is it not moving?"** and click a square with a train on it. Routes
it *could* take are drawn on the track; reasons for the rest are listed underneath. With unsaved edits
it says so; without, it stays quiet.

**5. Multi-select.** Shift-click a few squares; shift-drag a box; right-click → Selection → *Select
This Whole Row*. Then drag the group, Fill it, Delete it. **Undo after each — every one should be a
single step.**

**6. The size controls.** Right-click → Diagram → the `+` and `−` pair. Grow once, shrink once: you
should get back exactly what you started with. `−` refuses if the edges hold track.

**7. The new route editor.** Routes menu → the new editor. **Open an existing route, change nothing,
save. Then check its delays and decoder types survived.** Two separate data-loss bugs lived precisely
there.

**8. Build a condition by hand.** Add a condition, change its Kind to Accessory, set Protocol to DCC,
save. This path was completely stuck until the last review found it.

**9. Capture.** Tick capture in the new editor, throw some switches, and watch them appear. Then
switch the destination to *Into conditions* and do it again.

**10. Export a diagram.** `Layout` → `Save This Diagram as a Picture...` — the page you are looking
at, no question asked. (`Save Diagram as a Picture...` below it still asks, for any other page.)

## Stage D — Know about, nothing to do (10 min)

**16. A graceful stop now takes a moment longer to report itself finished** — and is telling the truth
for the first time. `isRunning()` was blind to a locomotive between deciding where to go and departing,
so the layout called itself stopped while a train was one instruction from moving, and every "is it
safe to edit" check believed it. If it feels *slow* rather than a moment, tell me.

**17. A Stop pressed while a train is mid-sleep still waits that sleep out** — bounded by your delay
setting, or 30 s for the yield wait. Much smaller than the indefinite hang it replaced, not zero.
Making it zero means interruptible waits everywhere.

**18. Nine entries removed from the 3.0.0 changelog** as bugs that only ever existed on this branch.
The judgement call is *"trains stopped moving even though routes were available"*.

**19. `Automation.md` is now the user guide**; the JSON and API reference moved to `AutomationAPI.md`.

**20. Removed from the diagram editor:** paste-entire-row, paste-entire-column, the four
shift-the-whole-diagram items, and `Shift+C`/`Shift+R`. Multi-select replaces them.

**21. The condition editor offers only accessory and feedback kinds.** Others produce a permanently
false condition. Auto-locomotive is evaluated by routes but has no controls, so an existing one is
preserved read-only.

### What I could not test

Your Central Station. `testRouteReachesTheRails` now runs routes and checks the accessories — a DCC
command moves the DCC switch while its MM2 twin at the same address stays put, and delays are timed
rather than inspected — and reinstating the two original bugs fails three of its four tests.

But that proves a command was *formed and dispatched* correctly, not that hardware obeys. One
correction to something I said earlier: `DEBUG_SIMULATE_PACKETS` is not what makes those assertions
work — `setAccessoryState` updates the local state directly, so they hold with or without the echo.
What they really rest on is MM2 and DCC occupying separate UID spaces.
