How autonomy treats trains

A pasted train whose heading only a barred copy holds

OB-284AUT2-A1AUT3-B2TDY3-B2

At a square where arrivals from one side are barred (BottomMainA, east barred), a train can still face the barred way: reversed on the throttle, or turned by the Facing menu, it now stands on that copy and keeps its direction. A paste, the editor's Place and the Place Locomotive dialog still choose only ways trains may arrive in, so cutting that train and pasting it back turns it round.

  • Keep the heading on a paste too. The train then stands where autonomy won't start it, and Why not Moving? says why.
  • Keep turning it to a way trains may arrive in. That keeps your OB-270 rule "no impossible facing saved", but a paste changes the train's direction.

Recommendation: keep the heading. It makes the paste match the throttle and the Facing menu, and it meets your other OB-270 rule, "no train should inadvertently change direction when pasted".

"Keep the current direction" through a compulsory turn

OB-283

A journey that passes a square every train must turn at is turned there, and you don't see it happen. At a destination where trains may turn, answering "Yes – keep the current direction" can then leave the train facing the opposite way from how it started.

Your call: should "keep" mean the direction the train left in, counting the turns on the way?

AUT-C2

The "open that side" remedy at a terminus

GUI4-C3

A train facing a barred way is now told to "drive it off by hand, turn it round, or open that side under Trains May Arrive...". Recommendation: at a must-turn square, drop "open that side" and offer the other two remedies only. That needs one new sentence in eight languages.

Decided while you were away

I made these calls without you because each follows from a rule you've already set. Any of them can be reversed.

An import reads each train's facing from the old file

REG4-A1REG4-C1

The code said an old autonomy.json "cannot say" which way a train faces. It can: 2.8.1 sent a train only along the edges that start at its point, so the side those edges leave by is the way it drives. On a fresh upgrade of your railway, the import had stood the 2-8-4 at TopMainR1 facing south, but 2.8.1 only ever drove it north. The import now reads the facing from the file. Where the diagram can't hold that facing, it saves no impossible facing (your OB-270 rule) and names the square. It guesses only where the file can't say, and makes that guess after it has marked the squares where trains turn round. The log line no longer says "or just run them".

A rebuild puts a train back where it can start

TDY4-B1AUT4-B1

A train the railway had on a barred copy is now put back on the station copy of the same square that faces the same way, if there is one. At a square where trains may turn round, that is the turning copy. Before this fix, barring the side a standing train came in by meant autonomy refused it for good.

Why not Moving? on Manual

GUI4-C2AUT4-C1

On Manual it no longer says that a paused train "cannot be sent anywhere", because a pause only stops autonomy. The start rules apply to a train sent by hand only while autonomy runs, which is what the route check does.

The "facing a barred way" sentence, in eight languages

TDY4-C3GUI4-C1

Drive it off by hand, turn it round, or open that side under "Trains May Arrive..." in the autonomy editor." The earlier wording could be read either way, and the German and Polish said the opposite direction.
